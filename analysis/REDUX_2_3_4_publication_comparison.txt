5) Change -> Effect Causality (Research Explanation)
===============================================================================

1) Hard-negative calibration (largest effect)
   - Expanding and structuring negatives reduced false-high metadata on dissimilar controls.
   - Mechanism: contrastive baseline and/or percentile ranking relative to candidate pool.
   - Result: Known Dissimilar metadata collapsed to low single digits and stayed there.

2) Reporting mode choice by use-case
   - contrastive = stronger discrimination framing
   - rank_pct = retrieval/ranking stability framing
   - Same underlying similarities can look very different depending on reporting transform.
   - This explains why plausible custom-30 can appear "reasonable" in one branch and inflated in another.

3) Window weighting + coverage penalty
   - Recency weighting ({50:0.65,150:0.35}) and lambda=0.20 reduce stale-history and low-coverage confidence.
   - Effect is generally secondary to negative-pool/reporting-mode choices, but improves robustness.

4) Blended CAIS weights
   - Interpolating strict and mimic profiles reduced dependence on one extreme profile.
   - Improved ability to tune plausible behavior without sacrificing control separation.

5) Canonical execution discipline
   - REDUX_4’s canonical run block/file outputs are essential for reproducibility.
   - Without strict run discipline, mixed historical outputs can produce contradictory conclusions.
